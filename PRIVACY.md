# Privacy Policy for Clarity Finance

**Last updated: September 1, 2026**

Clarity Finance ("Clarity", "we", "our") is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. We believe your personal financial data belongs solely to you.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage
- **Local On-Device Storage**: All accounts, transactions, budget plans, and goals are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database.
- **No Third-Party Analytics / Tracking**: Clarity does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data.

## 2. Google Drive Cloud Backup
- If you choose to connect **Google Drive**, Clarity utilizes the secure `drive.appdata` scope to create and store private database backups (`clarity_backup.json`).
- **Private Application Folder**: Your backup files are stored exclusively in your own private Google Drive application space, completely isolated from your public or personal Drive files.
- **No Developer Access**: The developers of Clarity cannot access, view, or transfer your Google Drive backup files. Data transfers directly between your device and Google's secure servers.

## 3. Permissions
- **Biometric Authentication**: Used locally on your device to lock and unlock the app. Biometric credentials never leave your device.
- **Internet Access**: Used solely for Google Drive backup synchronization and push notifications.
